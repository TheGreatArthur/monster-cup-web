Events

Events show on the left of the play page. When one ends, the players are ranked and the coins of each place are paid at once. A battle event counts online wins; a contest ranks the best Digimon handed over from the bank, one per player; a collection counts the Digimon handed over. Digimon handed over stay with the event, and go back to their owners' banks only if the event is cancelled.
